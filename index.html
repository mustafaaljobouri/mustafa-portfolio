About

Multi-dimensional creative ✨ 🧠

That’s what I would say if you asked me about me. My passion for music, tech, and writing allows me to approach every project with a multi-dimensional lens. My work doesn’t start on a screen, it usually starts with a pen, paper, and a can of Diet Red Bull, where ideas are raw, fast, and unrestricted. It’s where I explore directions without limitations, focusing purely on planning the work before working the plan.

I don’t see creativity as just visuals. It’s thinking, structure, and intention working together to create something that not only looks good, but actually does something.
